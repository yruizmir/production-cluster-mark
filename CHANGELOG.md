# Changelog

What changed in each version of this extension, newest first.

## 0.2.0

- More ways to mark a production cluster: a strip along the top of the window, a PRODUCTION label with the cluster's name in the top bar and in the status bar, and a PRODUCTION row first under each production cluster in the navigator.
- Turn each mark on or off, and give each one a colour of its own, from a few presets or any colour you like. The window edge and the top strip each have a thickness of their own.
- All of it is under **Preferences → Extensions → production-cluster-mark**.

## 0.1.4

- Replaced the red triangle icon with a dark square framed in red, like a Lens window marked as production.

## 0.1.3

- Put the extension's name, Production Cluster Mark, large on its picture, where cropping cannot cut it off.

## 0.1.2

- Made the picture on the extension's card bigger.

## 0.1.1

- Linked the source repository, and a place to report problems from the extension's details in Lens.

## 0.1.0

- Created the extension.
- While a production cluster's tab is selected, a red edge runs around the whole window.
- Clusters named with `prod`, `production` or `prd` count as production automatically.
- Mark or unmark any cluster as production from its right-click menu in the navigator, or from the command palette.
